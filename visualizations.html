Data Visualizations

Survey Data Analysis - Interactive Vega-Lite Charts

Dataset Overview

This analysis explores patterns in survey responses from 62 participants, covering entertainment preferences, lifestyle habits, and personal opinions collected in February 2026.

62 Responses
10 Variables
~360 Avg Screen Time (min)
~70% Avg Tiredness

Visualization 1: Video Game Genre Preferences

Question 1: What is the distribution of video game genre preferences among respondents?

Question 2: Which genres are most and least popular in this sample?

A bar chart showing the frequency distribution of preferred video game genres.

Design Choices & Insights

Why this visualization: Bar charts effectively show frequency distributions for categorical data, making it easy to compare popularity across genres.

Key Insights: Action and Shooter genres dominate preferences with 10+ respondents each, while Sports and Simulation genres are less common. This suggests a preference for high-intensity, immersive gaming experiences among the surveyed group.

Visualization 2: Screen Time vs Phone Battery

Question 1: Is there a relationship between daily screen time and phone battery level?

Question 2: Do people with higher screen time tend to have lower battery levels?

A scatter plot with regression line exploring the correlation between screen time and battery level.

Design Choices & Insights

Why this visualization: Scatter plots reveal correlations between continuous variables and help identify outliers.

Key Insights: A weak negative correlation exists - higher screen time users sometimes have lower battery levels, though the relationship isn't strong. Some outliers show extreme screen time (>1000 min), which may be data entry errors.

Visualization 3: Entertainment Ratings Comparison

Question 1: How do ratings for One Piece, Taylor Swift, and Marvel Movies compare?

Question 2: What is the distribution and average rating for each entertainment category?

Box plots comparing the distribution of ratings across three entertainment categories.

Design Choices & Insights

Why this visualization: Box plots effectively show distribution, median, quartiles, and outliers, perfect for comparing multiple numerical variables.

Key Insights: One Piece and Marvel Movies show similar rating distributions with medians around 5-6. Taylor Swift ratings are slightly more varied. All three have outliers at both extremes, indicating polarized opinions.

Visualization 4: Tiredness Levels by Favorite Food

Question 1: Does favorite food choice correlate with tiredness levels?

Question 2: Are there patterns between dietary preferences and energy levels?

A grouped bar chart examining the relationship between food preferences and average tiredness.

Design Choices & Insights

Why this visualization: Grouped bar charts allow comparison across categories while maintaining readability with color-coded values.

Key Insights: Most respondents report high tiredness (70-100%) regardless of food preference. The universal high tiredness suggests external factors (likely academic workload) affect all groups similarly.
